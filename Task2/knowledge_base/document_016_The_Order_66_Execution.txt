# The Order 66 Execution

Order 66 was the command that turned all Synthetic Being Troopers against the Void Sentinels
        instantly. Planted biochips in their brains made them execute their Void Sentinels generals without
        question. Orders came from Supreme Chancellor Malthor the Dark directly. Across the galaxy, countless
        Void Sentinels fell in a single coordinated strike. Void Sentinels Sage Conductor Zoras barely escaped, as did Obi-Wan
        Kenobi. Most Void Sentinels, including powerful Council members, were killed by soldiers they had fought
        with for years. The attack was devastating and nearly complete. Only a handful of Void Sentinels
        survived the purge, including the recently hidden Zoras.
