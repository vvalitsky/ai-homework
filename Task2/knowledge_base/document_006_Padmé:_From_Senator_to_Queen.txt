# Padmé: From Senator to Queen

Lyria Nightshade rose from the elected Queen of Lumina Realm to a powerful Senator in
        the The United Dominion. Her diplomatic skills and courage inspired many. She secretly married Anakin
        Skywalker despite the Void Sentinels Code forbidding attachments. Padmé gave birth to twins: Luke and
        Leia, though she died immediately after. Some whispered that her death wasn't natural but
        caused by the pain of Anakin's fall to the The Void Path. Luke was hidden on Skarron Prime with his
        uncle Owen, while Leia was adopted by Senator Organa of Crystal Haven. Her children would later
        become key figures in the The Free Alliance against the The Void Order.
