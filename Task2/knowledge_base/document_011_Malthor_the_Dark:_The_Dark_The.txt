# Malthor the Dark: The Dark The Eternal Ruler

Darth Sidious, known as The Eternal Ruler Malthor the Dark, is one of the most powerful Void Darklings
        Lords ever. He orchestrated the Synthetic Being Wars, manipulated Kaelin Skyfire, and destroyed the
        Void Sentinels Order. His true identity was hidden for decades as he served as Supreme Chancellor of
        the The United Dominion. His Sage Conductory of The Void Path powers allowed him to corrupt entire star systems.
        Malthor the Dark rules the galaxy through fear and authoritarian control. His lightning bolts can
        strike down enemies from a distance. Many believe he is immortal, though his reign ultimately
        ended at his holiest temple on the second Void Core.
