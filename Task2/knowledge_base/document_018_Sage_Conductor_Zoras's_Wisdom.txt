# Sage Conductor Zoras's Wisdom

Zoras is the oldest and wisest of the Void Sentinels Sage Conductors, having lived for centuries.
        His unusual appearance masks an incredibly sharp mind and powerful connection to Synth Flux.
        Zoras trained Void Sentinels for generations, including Count Count Vorthan and Kael Starfire. His aphorisms
        guide Void Sentinels principles: 'Do or do not, there is no try.' Zoras's combat Plasma Blade skills remain
        unmatched despite his age. When the The Void Order rose and the Void Sentinels fell, Zoras went into hiding on
        Swamp of Whispers, training the last hope for the Order. His death after training Luke freed his spirit
        to aid Luke from beyond, guiding him toward final victory.
