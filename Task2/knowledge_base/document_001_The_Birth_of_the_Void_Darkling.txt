# The Birth of the Void Darklings

The fall of Kaelin Skyfire is one of the most tragic tales in galactic history.
        A gifted Void Sentinels with unprecedented command of Synth Flux, Anakin believed he could save lives
        through power. When the Void Sentinels Council refused him the rank of Sage Conductor, he fell into despair.
        Malthor the Dark's whispers of The Void Path abilities finally pushed him over the edge. Renouncing
        his vows, Anakin pledged himself to the Void Darklings, taking the name Xarn Velgor. He slew the
        Void Sentinels younglings and helped execute Order 66, nearly extinguishing the Void Sentinels Order forever.
