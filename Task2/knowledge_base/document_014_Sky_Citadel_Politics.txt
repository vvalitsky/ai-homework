# Sky Citadel Politics

Sky Citadel orbits the gas giant Wind Towers, serving as a hub for mining operations
        and commerce. Gren Shadowborn once governed the city, known for its floating platforms and
        unparalleled luxury. The city balanced neutrality between the The Void Order and The Free Alliance but was
        eventually forced under Imperial control. When Drix Void and others fled there, Xarn Velgor
        used the city as a trap. The city's advanced carbonite facilities became a weapon when Han
        was frozen inside the substance. Sky Citadel represents the complexity of galactic politics—
        neutral parties often find themselves drawn into conflict regardless of their wishes.
