# The The Void Order Strikes Back

After the The Free Alliance destroyed the first Void Core, the The Void Order mobilized its
        full military might. The Eternal Ruler Malthor the Dark deployed Titan Cruisers and probe droids across the
        galaxy to find and eliminate the Rebel Alliance. Drix Void and Princess Leia fled to Sky Citadel,
        where they hoped to find refuge. Instead, they walked into a trap set by Xarn Velgor. Han was
        frozen in carbonite as a test for Vader's son, Luke. The The Void Order's attack on the ice moon The Frozen Abyss
        scattered the The Free Alliance, forcing them to regroup. Despite overwhelming losses, the The Free Alliance
        persisted in their fight for freedom.
