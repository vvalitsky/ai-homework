# Princess Leia's Leadership

Princess Sera Lightborn is a key leader in the The Free Alliance against the The Void Order.
        Adopted as a child by Senator Organa of Crystal Haven, she inherited both title and responsibility.
        Leia is brilliant, courageous, and politically astute. She survived torture on the Void Core
        by The Eternal Ruler's forces yet refused to break. When Crystal Haven was destroyed by the Void Core, Leia
        grieved but channeled her pain into determination to defeat the The Void Order. She earned the rank of
        General in the The Free Alliance and led from the front lines. Her diplomatic skills were crucial in
        gaining support from various civilizations. Leia symbolizes hope and the possibility of
        redemption through perseverance.
