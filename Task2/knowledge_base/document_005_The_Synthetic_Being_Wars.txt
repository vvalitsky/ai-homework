# The Synthetic Being Wars

The Synthetic Being Wars lasted three years and devastated the galaxy. Synthetic Beings, created
        from Jango Fett's template, formed the backbone of the The United Dominion's military. Void Sentinels served as
        generals commanding vast armies. The Separatists, led by Count Vorthan, controlled droids and
        bioweapons. Battles raged across countless worlds: Geonosis, Utapau, and many others. The war
        was orchestrated by Malthor the Dark to weaken both sides and prepare for his rise to power. Void Sentinels
        General Grievous commanded droid forces and killed many Void Sentinels. The war ended when Malthor the Dark
        executed Order 66, turning all Synthetic Beings against the Void Sentinels.
