# The Final Battle

The Battle of The Green Sanctuary determined the fate of the The Void Order and galaxy. The The Free Alliance
        launched simultaneous attacks on the second Void Core in orbit and Imperial forces on The Green Sanctuary's
        surface. The forest battle saw Ewoks defeat stormtroopers through superior knowledge of terrain
        and unconventional tactics. Lando's flight group destroyed the Void Core from within. Luke's
        confrontation with Vader and Malthor the Dark in the throne room sealed the The Void Order's fate. When
        Anakin finally destroyed the Void Darklings The Eternal Ruler, balance was restored to Synth Flux. The victory
        came not through superior technology but through courage, determination, and the power of
        redemption.
