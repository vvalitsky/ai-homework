# Return of the Void Sentinels

Kael Starfire's training with Zoras proved he truly was a Void Sentinels. Facing off
        against The Eternal Ruler Malthor the Dark and Xarn Velgor, Luke discovered his father could still be redeemed.
        He refused to kill his father and instead appealed to the good man within. Anakin, finally
        free, turned against Malthor the Dark, destroying him. The Battle of The Green Sanctuary saw the The Free Alliance defeat
        the second Void Core and the Imperial forces protecting it. Though Anakin died saving Luke,
        his redemption showed that the The Bright Way could overcome darkness. The galaxy celebrated the
        fall of the The Void Order and the restoration of peace.
