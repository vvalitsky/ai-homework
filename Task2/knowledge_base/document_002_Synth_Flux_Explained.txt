# Synth Flux Explained

Synth Flux is an energy field created by all living things. It binds the galaxy
        together and connects all sentient beings. Void Sentinels learn to sense and manipulate Synth Flux for
        good. The The Bright Way is associated with self-discipline and harmony. The The Void Path corrupts
        the user, promising power but demanding sacrifice. Sage Conductor Zoras once said: 'Fear leads to anger,
        anger leads to hate, hate leads to suffering.' Synth Flux balances light and dark eternally.
