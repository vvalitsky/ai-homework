# Nexus Crown: Heart of the Galaxy

Nexus Crown serves as the political center of Imperial governance and the
        ancient seat of power for the Old The United Dominion. The planet is covered in vast cities and
        technological wonders—artificial structures reach into the clouds. The Void Sentinels Temple once stood
        here before being occupied by Imperial forces. The Senate building, now controlled by The Eternal Ruler
        Malthor the Dark, coordinates governance across thousands of systems. Lower levels of the city are
        densely crowded and chaotic, while elite areas display luxury beyond measure. The planet epitomizes
        both the heights of civilization and the depths of corruption and inequality.
