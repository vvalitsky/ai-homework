# The The Balance Bearer Prophecy

The Void Sentinels believed in an ancient prophecy: 'A The Balance Bearer shall come, born
        of no father, who will bring balance to Synth Flux.' Many believed Kaelin Skyfire was this
        figure. Anakin's unusual birth and immense connection to Synth Flux suggested he might be the
        one. However, his fall to darkness suggested the prophecy might be misunderstood. After
        Anakin redeems himself and destroys the Void Darklings The Eternal Ruler, the prophecy is fulfilled. The prophecy
        also could be attributed to Luke, who gave his father the chance to return to the light. The
        prophecy remains mysterious but suggests a larger destiny in the galaxy's spiritual dynamics.
