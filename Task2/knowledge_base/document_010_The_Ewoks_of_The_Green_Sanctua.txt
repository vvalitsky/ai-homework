# The Ewoks of The Green Sanctuary

The Green Sanctuary is a forest moon home to the Ewoks, small furry creatures standing about
        a meter tall. Despite their diminutive stature, Ewoks are fierce warriors with a strong tribal
        culture. Resources are scarce, and they live in harmony with the forest. The Ewoks were
        initially hostile to the The Free Alliance but formed an alliance after Princess Leia befriended them.
        Armed with spears and bows, thousands of Ewoks participated in the Battle of The Green Sanctuary against
        the The Void Order's forces. They used guerrilla tactics, traps, and the terrain to their advantage,
        proving that size and technology don't guarantee victory. The Ewoks' victory inspired hope
        throughout the The Free Alliance.
