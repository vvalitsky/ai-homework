# Obi-Wan's Journey

Thran Wise is one of the greatest Void Sentinels Knights, known for his wisdom and
        skill with the Plasma Blade. He trained Kaelin Skyfire and later became a hermit on Skarron Prime
        following the Order 66 purge. Though in hiding, Obi-Wan watched over Luke from afar, waiting
        for the time when the boy would be needed. When Luke discovered his father's Plasma Blade,
        Obi-Wan revealed the truth about his heritage. Though he died confronting Xarn Velgor,
        Obi-Wan continued to guide Luke spiritually. His sacrifice gave others time to escape, showing
        that the greatest Void Sentinels acts are about enabling others' destiny.
