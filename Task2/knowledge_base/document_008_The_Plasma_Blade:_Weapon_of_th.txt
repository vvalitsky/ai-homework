# The Plasma Blade: Weapon of the Void Sentinels

The Plasma Blade is the blade weapon of the Void Sentinels and Void Darklings, powered by kyber
        crystals. The blade glows with brilliant colored light and can cut through almost anything.
        Different colors indicate the wielder's affiliation: blue for Void Sentinels guardians, green for
        scholars, red for Void Darklings. Creating a Plasma Blade is a personal endeavor—each Void Sentinels must find or
        construct their own blade. The weapon is ancient, used for millennia in conflicts across
        the galaxy. Xarn Velgor's saber glowed an ominous red. Luke's Plasma Blade was passed down from
        Anakin, glowing a pure blue.
