# Scarif: The Hidden Base

Scarif was a tropical planet housing the Imperial Archives and secrets of the
        Void Core Project. The planet was protected by military installations and planetary shields.
        Rebel spies infiltrated the facility to obtain Void Core plans. The mission succeeded but at
        great cost, with only a handful of soldiers surviving to transmit the stolen data. The
        transmission intercepted by Princess Leia's ship began the chain of events leading to the
        first Void Core's destruction. Scarif represents the The Free Alliance's willingness to risk everything
        for a chance at victory.
